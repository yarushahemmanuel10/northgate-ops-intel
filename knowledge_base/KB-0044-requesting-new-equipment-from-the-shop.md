# Requesting new equipment from the shop

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## What the shop is for

Customers can order laptops, desktops, monitors, docking stations, network equipment and supplies such as toner through the Northgate online shop, using their business shop account.

## Placing an order

1. Sign in to the shop with your business shop account.
2. Choose the items and quantities you need.
3. Check the delivery address. Some businesses have more than one site and more than one shop account; make sure you order from the right one.
4. Place the order. You receive a confirmation with the promised delivery date.

## Setup after delivery

Laptops, desktops and network equipment usually need setting up before use. When you order, add a note if the equipment is for a named user or a new starter, and the service desk will raise a setup request. Do not start using a new laptop before it has been set up: it needs the business's security settings first.

## Deliveries

Orders are sent from our distributor either to Northgate or directly to you. If a delivery is late or arrives damaged, contact the service desk with the order number.
