# How to raise a support ticket

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Who this is for

This guide is for staff at customer businesses who need help from the Northgate service desk.

## Choose the right route

How you contact us depends on how urgent the problem is:

- **The whole business, or a critical system, is down (P1):** phone the service desk. Out of hours, P1 calls reach the on-call engineer.
- **A major function is not working for many people (P2):** phone the service desk.
- **One person is affected, or there is a workaround (P3):** use the support portal or send an email.
- **A request or a question (P4):** use the support portal or send an email.

Phoning for urgent problems matters: an email about a site with no internet, for example, may never reach us.

## What to include

Whichever route you use, include:

- your name and your company
- what you were trying to do and what happened instead
- any error message, word for word, or a screenshot
- when it started and whether it worked before
- how many people are affected
- a good time and way to contact you

## What happens next

The service desk logs a ticket and gives it a priority. You receive a ticket reference by email. Quote it whenever you contact us about the same problem, so we do not open a duplicate.

## Service desk hours

The service desk is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00, except bank holidays. Outside those hours only P1 problems are handled, by the on-call engineer.
