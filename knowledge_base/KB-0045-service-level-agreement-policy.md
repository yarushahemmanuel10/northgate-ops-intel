# Service level agreement policy

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Northgate prioritises support tickets for managed IT support customers and the response and resolution times we commit to.

## Priorities

Every ticket is given one of four priorities when it is logged. The service desk sets the priority from what the customer tells us and may change it as more is learned, recording the reason on the ticket.

| Priority | Meaning | Response target | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Whole business or critical system down | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| P2 | Major function impaired for many users | 2 hours | 8 hours |
| P3 | Single user or workaround available | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| P4 | Request or question | 8 hours | 72 hours |

## How times are measured

- The clock starts when the ticket is opened.
- **Response** means an agent or engineer has started working on the ticket and has contacted the customer.
- **Resolution** means the problem is fixed or the request completed, and the ticket is closed.
- Times are measured in elapsed hours from opening, including evenings and weekends.

## Reporting

Northgate reports SLA performance by priority each month. A ticket that misses its response or resolution target counts as a breach of that target.

## Customer responsibilities

To help us meet these targets, customers should report P1 and P2 problems by phone, give accurate information when reporting a problem, and make staff available when an engineer needs access to a person or a device.
