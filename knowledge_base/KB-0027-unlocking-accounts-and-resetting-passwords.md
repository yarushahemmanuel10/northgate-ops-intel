# Unlocking accounts and resetting passwords

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Purpose

This guide covers what to do when a user is locked out of their account or cannot remember their password.

## Confirm identity

Before changing anything, make sure the person asking is the account holder. Check them against the customer's authorised contacts or ring them back on the number we hold. A reset requested by email should be followed up by phone. Write down on the ticket how the identity check was done.

## Locked accounts

Look the user up in the admin centre. If the account is locked because of repeated wrong passwords, unlock it. Ask the user whether those attempts were theirs. If the user says they were not, stop and treat the account as possibly compromised.

## Forgotten passwords

Reset the password and tick the option forcing a new password at next sign-in. Read the temporary password to the user over the phone; do not send it by email. Remind them that passwords must be at least 12 characters and that multi-factor authentication remains switched on. Stay on the call until they have signed in successfully.

## Self-service

If the customer uses self-service password reset, check the user is enrolled and walk them through it, so they can reset their own password next time without calling.

## Account keeps locking

An account that locks again shortly after being unlocked usually has an old password stored on another device. A phone's mail app or a second laptop are the usual suspects. Ask the user to update the password everywhere.
