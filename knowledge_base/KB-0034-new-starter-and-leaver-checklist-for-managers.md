# New starter and leaver checklist for managers

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Who this is for

Managers at customer businesses who are welcoming a new member of staff or saying goodbye to one.

## New starters

Tell the service desk as early as you can, through the portal or by email, with:

- the person's full name, job title and start date
- the team they are joining, so we can copy the team's access
- any extra systems, shared mailboxes or shared drives they need
- whether they need a laptop, monitor or other equipment

If equipment is needed, order it early: deliveries are not always quick, and new equipment needs setting up before the first day.

On the first day, give the new starter the sign-in details we sent you. They will be asked to set a password of at least 12 characters and to register an authenticator app on their phone.

## Leavers

Tell the service desk the leaving date as soon as it is known. On that date we will:

- disable the account and sign the person out everywhere
- remove their access to shared drives, mailboxes and systems
- turn their mailbox into a shared mailbox that you can open for 30 days
- remove licences that are no longer needed

If someone must lose access immediately, phone the service desk and say so.

## Equipment

Collect laptops, phones and other equipment from leavers and return them to us, so we can wipe them and re-use or dispose of them safely.
