# Internet down at a site (old version)

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## When to use this article

Use it when a whole office has lost its internet connection.

## How the customer should report it

Ask the customer to email the service desk mailbox with the site name and the time the connection dropped. The service desk will pick up the email and log a ticket.

## Steps for the customer

1. Switch the router off at the wall, wait a moment, and switch it back on.
2. Wait for the lights on the router to settle and test the connection again.
3. If the internet is still down, email the service desk to say the restart did not help.

## Common causes

Most outages at small sites are caused by the router locking up, a power cut at the site, or a fault on the carrier's line. Restarting the router clears a locked-up router, which is why customers are asked to try it first. If the lights on the router do not come back at all after a restart, the router may have failed or the site may have lost power to the comms cabinet.

## What to include in the email

Ask the customer to say whether phones are also affected, whether other businesses in the building have the same problem, and which lights are showing on the router.

## Steps for the service desk

1. Log the ticket from the customer's email.
2. Check the router in the monitoring console.
3. If the line is down, raise a fault with the carrier and email the customer the reference.
