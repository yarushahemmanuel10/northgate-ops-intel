# Working from home checklist

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Who this is for

Staff at customer businesses who work from home some or all of the time.

## Before your first day at home

- Make sure the remote access VPN client is installed on your work laptop and that you have connected to it at least once from the office.
- Check that multi-factor authentication works on your phone. If you are changing phone soon, contact the service desk before you switch, not after.
- Know which files live on the shared drive and which are in the cloud file library. Cloud files work without the VPN; shared drive files need it.

## Every day

1. Connect to your home network.
2. Open the VPN client and connect if you need the shared drive or internal systems.
3. Save work to the shared drive or cloud library, not only to your desktop, so it is backed up.

## Security at home

- Lock your laptop whenever you step away from it.
- Do not let family members use your work laptop.
- Use your home network rather than public Wi-Fi where possible.
- Report lost or stolen equipment straight away, whatever the time.

## Printing at home

Office printers are not available from home. If you need to print regularly at home, ask your manager whether a home printer can be supplied.

## When something goes wrong

If the VPN will not connect or multi-factor sign-in fails, contact the service desk using the routes in the support hours article.
