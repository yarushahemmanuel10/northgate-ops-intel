# Looking after your laptop battery and power

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Why it matters

Many reports of laptops that will not start turn out to be a flat battery or a failed power adapter. A little care avoids lost time.

## Everyday care

- Use the power adapter that came with the laptop, or an approved replacement from the shop. Cheap chargers can damage the battery.
- Do not leave the laptop in a hot car or in direct sunlight.
- Keep air vents clear: avoid using the laptop on a bed or sofa where vents are blocked.
- Shut the laptop down properly at the end of the week so updates can finish.

## If the laptop will not turn on

1. Plug in the adapter and check the charging light.
2. Try a different wall socket.
3. Hold the power button down until all lights go off, release it, and press it once.
4. If there is still no sign of life, phone the service desk. Engineers can test with a spare adapter.

## Battery wearing out

Batteries lose capacity as they age. If the laptop only lasts a short time unplugged, ask the service desk to check the battery's health. A replacement battery or adapter can be ordered through the shop.

## Swollen batteries

If the laptop case bulges, the trackpad lifts or the laptop no longer sits flat, the battery may be swelling. Stop using it, unplug it and contact the service desk. Do not try to remove the battery yourself.
