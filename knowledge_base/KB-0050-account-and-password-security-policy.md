# Account and password security policy

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Purpose

This policy sets the minimum account security standards for every customer account Northgate manages.

## Passwords

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters.
- Passwords must not be shared, written down near the computer, or sent by email.
- Temporary passwords set by the service desk must be changed at the next sign-in.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required on every account. Exceptions are not granted for individual users.

## Verifying users

The service desk verifies the caller before resetting a password, unlocking an account, or changing multi-factor authentication methods, using the customer's list of authorised contacts or a call back to a number already held.

## New starters

Accounts are created only on a request from the customer's manager or authorised contact. Sign-in details are sent to the manager, not to a personal email address.

## Leavers

Accounts are disabled on the leaving date. The line manager can access the leaver's mailbox, converted to a shared mailbox, for 30 days. Licences are removed once they are no longer needed.

## Suspected compromise

Any account that shows signs of compromise is blocked, its sessions revoked and its password reset, before the investigation continues.
