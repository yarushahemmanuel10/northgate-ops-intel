# Printer and copier basics

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Printing

Office printers and copiers are shared over the office network. They appear on your computer automatically when it is set up by the service desk. If a printer is missing, see the article on adding a printer.

## Before reporting a printer problem

- Check the printer's screen for a message such as a paper jam, low toner or an open tray, and clear it.
- Check whether colleagues can print. If nobody can, the printer itself or the network has a problem; say so when you report it.
- Do not keep sending the same job. Repeated jobs pile up in the queue and print all at once when the problem is fixed.

## Scanning

Copiers can scan straight to your email. Choose scan to email, pick your name from the address book or type your address, and press start. If scans stop arriving, check your junk folder, then contact the service desk.

## Consumables

Toner and other supplies can be ordered through the shop. Order before the printer runs out completely.

## Printing from home

Office printers cannot be reached from home.
