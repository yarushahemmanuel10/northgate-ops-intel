# Support hours and contact routes

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Service desk hours

The service desk is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 (UK time), excluding bank holidays.

## Out-of-hours cover

Outside service desk hours, P1 problems are covered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, by an on-call engineer. P2, P3 and P4 tickets raised out of hours are picked up when the service desk next opens.

## How to report a problem

| Priority | How to report |
|---|---|
| P1 | Phone |
| P2 | Phone |
| P3 | Support portal or email |
| P4 | Support portal or email |

Urgent problems must be phoned in. Email and the portal are checked regularly during service desk hours, but a message about an urgent problem could wait in a queue, and a site with no internet may not be able to send email at all.

## Who can contact us

Each customer gives us a list of authorised contacts. Requests that change access, such as password resets, new accounts or access to shared mailboxes, are only carried out for verified users or authorised contacts.
