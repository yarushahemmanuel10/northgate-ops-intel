# Data handling and personal data policy

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Purpose

Northgate handles personal data on behalf of its customers when providing IT support: names, email addresses and phone numbers of customer staff, and any personal data held in the systems we support. This policy sets out how support staff should handle it, in line with UK GDPR principles.

## Principles

- **Data minimisation.** Record only what is needed to resolve a ticket. Do not paste personal data, such as staff home addresses or payroll details, into ticket notes.
- **Purpose limitation.** Use personal data only to provide the support requested, not for any other purpose.
- **Security.** Never send passwords by email. Verify callers before making account changes. Lock your screen when you leave your desk.
- **Access.** Look at a customer's data only when a ticket requires it.

## Remote sessions and screenshots

Ask users to close windows showing personal or confidential information before a remote session starts. Do not keep screenshots that show personal data after the ticket is closed.

## Tools that process ticket text

Personal data in ticket text must be masked before the text is passed to any automated analysis or AI tool.

## Incidents

If personal data may have been lost, exposed or accessed without permission, for example through a compromised account or a lost device, tell the service desk lead immediately. The customer may need to decide whether to report the incident, and we help them establish the facts.
