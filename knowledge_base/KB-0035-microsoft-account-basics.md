# Microsoft account basics

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## What your work account gives you

Your work account signs you in to email, calendar, chat, the cloud file library and the office apps. One password and one multi-factor registration cover all of them.

## Email and storage

- Your mailbox has a storage limit. When it is nearly full you will see a warning. Empty Deleted Items and Junk Email regularly and avoid keeping large attachments you no longer need.
- Some businesses use an online archive. Older messages move there automatically and still appear in your folder list.

## Shared mailboxes

Shared mailboxes, such as an accounts or enquiries mailbox, appear under your own mailbox in Outlook once you have been given access. Access must be requested by your manager. If you can see a shared mailbox in webmail but not in Outlook, restart Outlook before contacting the service desk.

## Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least 12 characters.
- Multi-factor authentication is required on every account.
- If you are locked out, phone the service desk. We will check who you are before resetting anything.
- If your business uses self-service password reset, you can reset your own password from the sign-in page once you are enrolled.

## Using your account on your phone

You can add your work email and chat to your phone. If you change your password, update it on your phone too, or your account may lock.
