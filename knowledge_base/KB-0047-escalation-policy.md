# Escalation policy

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Purpose

This policy explains when a ticket moves from the first-line service desk to an engineer, and when it is reported to management.

## First-line escalation

The first-line service desk aims to resolve as many tickets as possible on first contact. A ticket not resolved at first line by 50% of its resolution target moves to an engineer. For example, a P3 ticket with a resolution target of 24 hours is escalated if it is still unresolved at first line after half of that time.

An agent may also escalate earlier when it is clear the problem needs an engineer, for example a hardware fault needing a visit.

## Management escalation

Any P1 still open 2 hours after it was opened is reported to the operations director, who checks that the right people are working on it and that the customer is being kept informed.

## Keeping the customer informed

When a ticket is escalated, the customer is told who now owns it. For P1 and P2 tickets, the customer receives updates by phone.

## Customer escalation

If a customer is unhappy with how a ticket is being handled, they can ask the service desk to escalate it to the service desk lead. The request and the outcome are recorded on the ticket.
