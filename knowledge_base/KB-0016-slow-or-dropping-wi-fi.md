# Slow or dropping Wi-Fi

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Symptoms

Wi-Fi is slow in parts of the office, or devices keep dropping the connection. The wired network and the internet connection itself are fine.

## Narrow it down

1. Ask which rooms or desks are affected and whether the problem is worse at particular times of day.
2. Ask whether it affects all devices or only some. One device dropping out is usually a driver or settings problem on that device.
3. Check the wireless controller or access point dashboard for access points that are offline or showing errors.

## Common causes and fixes

**Congested channels.** Neighbouring offices' networks can crowd the same channels. Move access points to less congested channels from the controller and update their firmware.

**A failed access point.** If one access point is offline, devices in that area connect to a more distant one and get a weak signal. Replace the failed access point. A visit may be needed.

**Coverage gaps.** If the office layout has changed, for example new partitions or a storeroom moved, coverage may no longer reach some desks. Book a survey and propose an extra access point through the shop.

**Device drivers.** For a single device, update the wireless driver and forget and re-join the network.
