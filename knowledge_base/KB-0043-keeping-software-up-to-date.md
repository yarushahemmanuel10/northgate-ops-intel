# Keeping software up to date

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Why updates matter

Updates fix security holes that attackers use. Computers that fall behind are the easiest target in any business.

## How updates reach your computer

Northgate manages updates for Windows and the office apps through device management. Updates download in the background and install when you restart.

## What you need to do

- Restart your computer when asked, ideally at the end of the day.
- Leave laptops plugged in and switched on overnight now and then, so updates can finish.
- Do not postpone restarts again and again. After a while the computer will restart on its own.

## Installing new software

Do not install software yourself from websites. Request it through the service desk portal; your manager will be asked to approve it. Approved software can often be installed from the software portal in a few clicks.

## If updates keep failing

If your computer asks to restart for updates every day and never finishes, contact the service desk. A nearly full disk is a common cause, and we can help free space.
