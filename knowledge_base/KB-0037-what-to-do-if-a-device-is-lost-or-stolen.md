# What to do if a device is lost or stolen

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Report it straight away

If a work laptop, phone or tablet is lost or stolen, phone the service desk immediately. Out of hours, treat it as urgent: a lost device holding customer data can become a data breach, and the on-call route exists for exactly this kind of problem.

## What we do

1. Sign the user out of all sessions and reset their password.
2. Use device management to lock the device and, if it is unlikely to be recovered, wipe it remotely.
3. Remove the device's access to email and files.
4. Check the sign-in logs for any use of the account after the device went missing.
5. Record what data the device could reach, so the customer can decide whether anything must be reported.

## What you should do

- Report the theft to the police if the device was stolen, and give us the crime reference.
- Tell your manager.
- Change any personal passwords that were saved on the device.

## Getting a replacement

Your manager can order a replacement through the shop. New devices are set up with your account and the business's standard apps before they reach you.
