# Mailbox over its size limit

> Synthetic document. Northgate IT Services Ltd is a fictional company; this text was written for a portfolio prototype.

## Purpose

This guide helps when a user's mailbox has reached its storage limit.

## What the user notices

Outlook warns that the mailbox is full or nearly full. The user may be unable to send, and eventually unable to receive, email. External senders can get a bounce saying the mailbox is full. Phones may quietly stop syncing.

## Check first

Make sure the warning is about the user's own mailbox and not a shared mailbox they can open. Find out whether an online archive is enabled, because moving old mail there is the fastest fix. If the user keeps mail in a local data file, do not delete anything from it without their permission.

## Fix

Look at the mailbox size against its limit. Ask the user to empty Deleted Items and Junk Email. Sort Sent Items by size and help them remove or archive large messages. Where an online archive exists, apply an archive policy so older items move across automatically, and show the user where the archive appears. If more space is genuinely needed and the manager agrees, increase the quota and note it on the ticket. Finish with a test message to confirm sending works.

## If space does not free up

Deleted items may still be held for recovery for a while. Check again after the next sync, or ask an engineer to look at recoverable items. For mailboxes of staff who have left, use the leaver guide rather than raising the quota.

## Escalate

Escalate if business-critical email has stopped and the mailbox cannot be cleared quickly, or if many mailboxes report the same issue.
